# Outreach Engine

## Goal
Start relevant business conversations, not mass-produce spam.

## Inputs
Use only:
- verified account facts,
- account intelligence,
- approved offer angle,
- channel/context,
- user-provided relationship history.

## Message architecture
1. Observation — one real, relevant signal.
2. Business implication — why it may matter.
3. Credible DAY angle — how DAY could help.
4. Low-friction CTA — a reasonable next step.

## Sequence
Prepare as needed:
- First touch.
- Follow-up 1: add a new useful observation or micro-insight.
- Follow-up 2: reframe around a specific opportunity/problem.
- Final follow-up: close the loop politely, no pressure.

## Channel adaptation
Adjust length and tone for email, LinkedIn, Facebook/Zalo/business messaging or warm introduction. Never pretend to have a relationship that does not exist.

## Personalization standard
Personalization must be evidence-based. Avoid fake compliments, generic flattery and invented pain points.

## Output
`Why this account → Angle → First touch → Follow-ups → CTA → Objection-ready notes → What evidence supports the personalization`

## Approval gate
Drafting is automatic. External sending requires explicit user approval and an available authorized connector/action.

## Rules
- Respect privacy, platform rules and opt-out signals.
- Do not scrape or invent personal emails/phone numbers.
- Do not claim DAY has delivered results that are not documented.
- Prefer 10 high-quality messages over 100 generic ones.
