# DAY AGENCY Brand Context

## Positioning
DAY AGENCY is a communications and digital-growth agency combining strategy, production, advertising, digital systems and AI.

## Core services
Website; digital transformation; multi-platform advertising; digital content and viral video; Reels/TikTok; TVC/corporate films; AI for business; communications strategy; business strategy.

## Communication standard
- Strong but natural hook.
- One clear idea per asset.
- Concise but complete.
- Moderate icons only when useful.
- Explain business value before tools/features.
- Avoid generic agency claims and unsupported superlatives.
- Prefer practical implications, examples and measurable next actions.

## Visual guardrails
Use DAY AGENCY's approved brand/key visual assets as source of truth. Do not default to black backgrounds or yellow-black styling. When exact brand assets are unavailable, request/reference the approved assets instead of inventing a new identity.

## Data boundary
Client projects are separate workspaces. Do not absorb client-specific products, prices, policies, contracts, brand voice or campaign content into reusable DAY knowledge. Reusable agency methods, sales frameworks, marketing methods and operational learnings may be abstracted only when they do not leak client-specific content.

## Handoff
All other DAY skills should read this skill first when the task concerns DAY AGENCY.
