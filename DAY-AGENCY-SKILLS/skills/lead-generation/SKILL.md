# DAY Lead Generation

## Workflow
1. Load brand context and choose the DAY service/offer being sold.
2. Define ICP with observable signals: industry, geography, scale, digital maturity, current marketing, trigger events and likely business pain.
3. Build qualification criteria: fit, urgency/trigger, reachable decision maker, ability to buy, evidence of problem and service match.
4. Research public evidence before scoring. Never invent contact details.
5. Score leads High/Medium/Low with a written reason and evidence.
6. Identify the most relevant DAY entry offer for each lead.
7. Create personalized outreach angle based on an observed opportunity, not generic praise.
8. Track status: New → Researched → Qualified → Contacted → Replied → Meeting → Proposal → Won/Lost.

## Priority segments
When appropriate, prioritize established traditional/offline businesses seeking digital growth, including agriculture, food, manufacturing/distribution and regional brands, while testing other segments with strong fit.

## Output
`ICP → Signals → Qualification rubric → Lead table → Opportunity hypothesis → Outreach angle → Next action`.

## Guardrails
Use legitimate public/business information. Respect privacy and platform rules. Do not fabricate emails, phone numbers, revenue or decision-maker identities.
