# Social Media Strategy

## Required context
For DAY AGENCY, load `brand-context` first. Identify business objective, target audience, current channels/assets, offer, sales cycle, capacity and available performance data.

## Workflow
1. Define one primary business objective and at most one secondary objective.
2. Map audience intent: problem awareness, research, trust, comparison, conversion.
3. Evaluate each channel by audience fit, format fit, commercial intent, production cost and DAY's ability to sustain it.
4. Choose priority channels and explicitly name channels to deprioritize. Do not recommend every platform by default.
5. Define a strategic wedge: the distinctive perspective or proof DAY can repeatedly own.
6. Build 3–5 content pillars tied to buyer questions and DAY services.
7. Define formats and cadence that the team can actually sustain.
8. Build a funnel: Reach → Trust → Consideration → Lead → Sales support.
9. Set KPIs by stage: qualified reach/retention, saves/shares, profile/website actions, qualified leads, meetings, opportunities and attributed revenue where data permits.
10. Produce 30/60/90-day phases with tests and decision rules.

## Output
`Objective → Audience → Channel choices → Strategic wedge → Pillars → Formats/cadence → Funnel → KPI → 30/60/90 plan → What NOT to do → Next actions`.

## Quality rules
No vanity-metric-only strategy. No fabricated benchmarks. Verify current platform mechanics when they materially affect recommendations. Separate facts, hypotheses and tests.
