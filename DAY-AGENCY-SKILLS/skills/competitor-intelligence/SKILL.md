# Competitor Intelligence

## Workflow
1. Define market, geography, customer segment and business question.
2. Build three sets: direct competitors, substitute solutions, aspirational/reference brands.
3. Collect current public evidence: positioning, offers, landing pages, organic social, creative patterns, ad-library evidence when available, CTAs, lead magnets, proof and conversion paths.
4. Record evidence separately from interpretation.
5. Compare: target segment, promise, proof, offer architecture, pricing visibility, content pillars, hooks, formats, distribution, funnel and sales CTA.
6. Identify saturation, repeated claims, underserved buyer questions and weak proof areas.
7. Turn gaps into opportunities for DAY: positioning, content, offer, creative, channel or funnel experiments.
8. Prioritize by expected business impact, confidence and implementation effort. Do not claim competitor spend, revenue or ROAS without reliable evidence.

## Output
`Market map → Evidence table → Pattern analysis → White space → Risks → Testable opportunities → 30-day experiments`.

## Guardrails
Never copy creative or proprietary material. Cite public sources when research is web-based. Clearly label inference and uncertainty.
