# DAY Content Engine

## Workflow
1. Load DAY brand context and current strategy.
2. Translate buyer problems and objections into 3–5 content pillars.
3. Give each pillar a job: attract, educate, prove, differentiate or convert.
4. Build repeatable series rather than isolated post ideas.
5. For each idea define: insight, hook, proof/example, body structure, CTA, best format and repurpose paths.
6. Balance content across top/mid/bottom funnel and DAY's service priorities.
7. Create weekly/monthly calendar based on realistic production capacity.
8. Review performance and recycle winning concepts with new angles instead of blindly increasing volume.

## DAY writing rules
Hook strong but natural. Clear idea. Concise and complete. Icons moderate. Avoid filler, generic AI phrasing and unsupported claims. Explain why the audience should care before listing services.

## Output
`Pillars → Series → Idea bank → Hook bank → Calendar → Repurposing map → CTA map → Measurement/iteration plan`.
