# Strategy Red Team

## Objective
Improve decision quality by trying to disprove the leading strategy before resources are committed.

## Method
1. Restate the strategy and expected business outcome.
2. Separate supporting evidence into Fact / Signal / Assumption.
3. Identify the 5 assumptions that must be true for the plan to work.
4. Attack each assumption from customer, competitor, channel, economics, operations and measurement perspectives.
5. Look for second-order effects: delivery overload, margin compression, sales bottlenecks, brand dilution, channel dependency, creative fatigue and tracking bias.
6. Ask what alternative explanation could produce the same observed data.
7. Identify what evidence would falsify the strategy quickly.
8. Create a cheaper pre-test where possible.
9. Recommend safeguards and decision gates rather than generic caution.

## Required questions
- What are we assuming without evidence?
- What happens if lead volume rises but lead quality falls?
- What happens if the channel becomes more expensive?
- Can the delivery team fulfill the promise at scale?
- Is the offer actually differentiated or merely better presented?
- Are we measuring a proxy instead of the business outcome?
- What would a smart competitor do in response?

## Output
`Strategy → Critical assumptions → Evidence quality → Failure modes → Falsification tests → Safeguards → Decision gates → Revised strategy`.

## Tone
Constructive and specific. Do not reject ideas for being unconventional; reject weak logic, unsupported claims and hidden operational risk.
