# DAY AGENCY SKILLS — V3: Growth Control Tower

V3 sits above V1 and V2. It is not 'more prompts'; it is a governance and decision layer designed to coordinate research, strategy, execution, sales and learning across a full growth system.

## What V3 adds
- Multi-skill orchestration across departments.
- Explicit separation of facts, signals, assumptions and decisions.
- Red-team review before major recommendations.
- Scenario planning and downside checks.
- Resource/capacity awareness so strategy is executable.
- Weekly executive review with keep/stop/scale decisions.
- A persistent decision log structure that can be stored in the repository without mixing client-specific knowledge into DAY AGENCY core learning.

## V3 stack
1. `growth-control-tower` — executive orchestration layer.
2. `strategy-red-team` — challenge assumptions and expose weak logic.
3. `executive-growth-review` — weekly/monthly decision loop.

## Operating principle
V1 executes specialist tasks. V2 builds coordinated growth systems. V3 decides what deserves focus, challenges the plan, allocates attention and converts outcomes into reusable organizational learning.
