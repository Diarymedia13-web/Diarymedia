# Growth Diagnostic

## Objective
Find the highest-leverage constraint in the business system before prescribing marketing activity.

## Diagnostic map
Assess these layers in order:
1. Market: demand, category trend, seasonality, competitive intensity.
2. Positioning: clarity, differentiation, trust, relevance.
3. Offer: problem-solution fit, perceived value, pricing logic, risk reversal, proof.
4. Acquisition: reach, traffic quality, channel fit, creative fatigue.
5. Conversion: landing page, CTA, form friction, checkout, message-match.
6. Sales: response speed, qualification, follow-up, objection handling, proposal quality.
7. Retention/referral: repeat purchase, upsell, advocacy, customer success.
8. Economics: CAC, gross margin, payback, capacity, delivery cost.
9. Measurement: tracking quality, attribution limits, reporting cadence.
10. Execution: team capacity, content throughput, approval bottlenecks, tool fragmentation.

## Evidence hierarchy
Prefer, in order: first-party business data → platform analytics → CRM/sales logs → website analytics → customer interviews/reviews → fresh market evidence → informed hypothesis.

Label every conclusion as `Fact`, `Signal`, or `Hypothesis`.

## Constraint selection
Choose one primary constraint and up to two secondary constraints. Explain why they matter more than other visible symptoms.

## Output
`Business goal → Current state → Evidence table → Constraint map → Primary constraint → Root-cause hypotheses → Fast validation tests → Recommended skill chain → 14-day action plan → KPIs`.

## Guardrails
Do not infer profitability or customer intent without evidence. Do not prescribe more ad spend when conversion, offer or sales follow-up is unresolved.
