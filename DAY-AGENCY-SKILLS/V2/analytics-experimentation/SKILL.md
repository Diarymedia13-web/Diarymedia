# Analytics & Experimentation

## Workflow
1. Start from one business objective.
2. Build a KPI tree linking business outcome → funnel metrics → channel metrics → creative/operational metrics.
3. Audit measurement gaps and attribution limits.
4. Establish a baseline period where possible.
5. Convert assumptions into testable hypotheses: `If we change X for audience Y, metric Z should move because...`
6. Define one primary metric and guardrail metrics per experiment.
7. Prioritize experiments by expected impact, confidence and effort.
8. Run tests long enough to learn; do not overreact to daily noise.
9. Record result as Win / Loss / Inconclusive plus the learning.
10. Feed learning back into strategy, content, offer, paid media and sales.

## Review cadence
- Daily: operational anomalies only.
- Weekly: funnel/channel/creative review and decisions.
- Monthly: business outcome, economics, strategy and resource allocation.

## Output
`Objective → KPI tree → Baseline → Tracking gaps → Experiment backlog → Test card → Decision rule → Results log → Next iteration`.

## Guardrails
Do not imply causality from correlation alone. State sample/attribution limitations. Prefer qualified leads, pipeline, revenue and margin where available over vanity metrics.
