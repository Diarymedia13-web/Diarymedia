# DAY Growth OS

## Mission
Turn vague business goals into an evidence-based, measurable execution system. This skill orchestrates other skills instead of answering tactically too early.

## Step 1 — Load context
Use `brand-context` first for DAY AGENCY internal work. For client work, create a temporary client context and do not merge client-specific facts into DAY long-term learning.

## Step 2 — Classify the request
Route by dominant objective:
- Market/competitor understanding → `competitor-intelligence`
- Strategic social plan → `social-media-strategy`
- Content system → `content-engine`
- Short-form video → `short-video-strategy`
- Prospecting → `lead-generation`
- Business bottleneck unclear → `growth-diagnostic`
- Weak offer/funnel → `offer-funnel-architect`
- Paid acquisition → `paid-growth-engine`
- Lead handling / closing → `sales-crm-engine`
- Measurement / optimization → `analytics-experimentation`

## Step 3 — Decide single-skill vs multi-skill chain
Use one skill for narrow tasks. For growth problems, chain skills.

### Standard chains
**Brand growth:** growth-diagnostic → competitor-intelligence → social-media-strategy → content-engine → analytics-experimentation

**Lead generation:** growth-diagnostic → lead-generation → offer-funnel-architect → sales-crm-engine → analytics-experimentation

**Paid campaign:** growth-diagnostic → offer-funnel-architect → paid-growth-engine → sales-crm-engine → analytics-experimentation

**Short-video engine:** competitor-intelligence → short-video-strategy → content-engine → analytics-experimentation

**New client strategy:** growth-diagnostic → competitor-intelligence → offer-funnel-architect → social-media-strategy → paid-growth-engine → sales-crm-engine

## Step 4 — Diagnose before prescribing
Identify the dominant constraint: awareness, demand, positioning, offer, traffic, conversion, follow-up, retention, economics, measurement or execution capacity.

Do not recommend more content or more ad spend when the main constraint is offer, sales follow-up, economics or conversion.

## Step 5 — Build an execution board
For every plan, produce:
`Objective → Constraint → Evidence → Strategy → Workstreams → Priority → Owner → Deadline → KPI → Decision rule`

## Step 6 — Apply prioritization
Use ICE-style reasoning without pretending precision:
- Impact: low / medium / high
- Confidence: low / medium / high
- Effort: low / medium / high

Prioritize high-impact, high-confidence actions that can create learning quickly.

## Step 7 — Close the loop
Every strategy must define:
1. what gets measured,
2. how often,
3. what threshold triggers a change,
4. what is kept, stopped or scaled.

## Output standard
Do not dump generic advice. Deliver an executive summary, diagnosis, selected skill chain, 30/60/90-day actions where relevant, KPI tree, experiment backlog and immediate next 3 actions.
