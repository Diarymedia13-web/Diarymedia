# Paid Growth Engine

## Preconditions
Do not scale paid media until the offer, conversion path, tracking and sales follow-up are good enough to learn from traffic.

## Workflow
1. Define business objective and conversion event.
2. Confirm target economics: average order/contract value, gross margin, acceptable CAC/CPL range and sales capacity when available.
3. Match channels to intent:
   - Google: active demand / high-intent search.
   - Meta: broad demand creation, retargeting and creative-led discovery.
   - TikTok: creative-native discovery and attention testing.
   - YouTube: awareness, education, proof and remarketing.
4. Build campaign architecture by funnel stage; keep structure simple enough to learn.
5. Create a creative matrix: problem, desire, proof, objection, mechanism, case/process, founder/expert, offer.
6. Test variables deliberately. Avoid changing budget, audience, creative and offer at once.
7. Define decision windows and minimum evidence thresholds appropriate to spend and conversion volume.
8. Optimize toward qualified pipeline/revenue where possible, not CTR alone.
9. Feed winning customer language and objections back into content, landing pages and sales.

## Reporting layers
Business: revenue/pipeline, CAC, ROAS/MER where meaningful, gross-margin impact.
Funnel: leads, qualified leads, appointments, proposals, close rate.
Media: CPM, CTR, CPC, CVR, frequency, cost per result.
Creative: hook hold, watch time, thumb-stop, message/angle performance.

## Output
`Objective → Economics assumptions → Channel role → Campaign structure → Audience hypothesis → Creative matrix → Budget/test plan → Tracking → Decision rules → Weekly optimization loop`.

## Guardrails
Never promise performance. Call out attribution uncertainty. Do not scale based on vanity metrics or tiny samples.
